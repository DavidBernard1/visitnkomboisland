About Nkombo Island

Welcome to Nkombo Island, a beautiful destination in Rwanda surrounded by the peaceful waters of Lake Kivu. Nkombo offers visitors a unique experience of nature, local culture, community life and breathtaking lakeside scenery.

Come and discover the island, meet its welcoming people, experience its traditions and enjoy the beauty of Lake Kivu.
